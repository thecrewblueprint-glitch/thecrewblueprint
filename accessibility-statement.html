Accessibility Statement

Last updated: September 10, 2026

The Crew Blueprint is intended to be usable by a broad range of learners, including people who use keyboards, assistive technology, zoom, reflow, reduced-motion settings, or alternative input methods.

Our Commitment

We are working toward accessible public previews, account flows, navigation, learning pages, assessments, legal pages, and downloadable material. Accessibility is treated as an ongoing product requirement rather than a one-time certification.

Current Practices

  • semantic headings and landmarks on primary learner pages;
  • keyboard-operable navigation and controls;
  • visible focus treatment;
  • responsive layouts intended to reflow on smaller screens;
  • text alternatives for instructional media where applicable;
  • reduced-motion support in the primary style system;
  • accessible names for buttons, links, forms, and authentication actions.

Testing Scope

Automated structural checks are part of the release process, but automated checks do not prove complete WCAG or ADA compliance. Manual keyboard, focus-order, zoom/reflow, contrast, screen-reader, form, assessment, and mobile testing are required before the production release is treated as accessibility-reviewed.

Known Limitations

Some archived, historical, or staging-only material may not meet the same standard as the production learner experience and is not intended for the final WordPress package. Third-party authentication interfaces are also partly controlled by their provider.

Report an Accessibility Barrier

If you encounter a barrier, include the page, the feature involved, the assistive technology/browser if relevant, and what you were trying to accomplish. We will use that information to investigate and prioritize a fix.
