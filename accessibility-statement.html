Accessibility Statement

The Crew Blueprint is committed to making our training content accessible to as many people as possible, including people with disabilities.

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Our Commitment

We strive to follow widely recognized accessibility best practices and standards where feasible, and to continuously improve the usability of this site.

Steps We Are Taking

  • Using clear, consistent headings and structure.
  • Designing with sufficient color contrast where possible.
  • Providing descriptive link text and labels where practical.
  • Continuously reviewing content for clarity and readability.

Limitations and Ongoing Work

Despite our efforts, some content or features may not yet be fully accessible. We are working to improve these areas over time.

Contact Us About Accessibility

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of this site, or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us. Let us know the page, the issue you encountered, and the assistive technology you are using, if applicable.
