Production Atlas

Production Atlas is a public-safe scouting tool for live event workers who want to research where production work is happening, when events take place, who produces them, and which companies may be connected to the work.

It helps stagehands, technicians, freelancers, and small labor companies identify festivals, venues, producers, vendors, and possible application routes without relying only on job boards.
