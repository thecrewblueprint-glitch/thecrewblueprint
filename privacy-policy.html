Privacy Policy

Introduction and Organizational Information

The Crew Blueprint is committed to responsible handling of personal information collected through our website, training platform, forms, customer interactions, and related business operations. We use personal information to provide educational content, manage user access, respond to inquiries, process transactions, maintain security, improve our website, and operate our business.

We do not currently have a designated Data Protection Officer. Privacy questions and requests may be sent to thecrewblueprint@gmail.com.

Training and safety note: The Crew Blueprint provides stagehand orientation, job-readiness, and safety-awareness education. It does not replace employer-provided safety training, site-specific instruction, union training, equipment certification, qualified supervision, or OSHA 10/30 Outreach training.

Services Provided

The Crew Blueprint provides online educational resources, training modules, course materials, downloadable references, checklists, quizzes, and job-readiness content for people learning about stagehand work, live event production, and related crew roles in concerts, festivals, arenas, stadiums, theaters, convention centers, corporate events, and other live production environments.

Our services may include free and paid educational content, account-based course access, learning progress features, digital downloads, contact forms, customer support, email communications, payment processing, and future membership or student-dashboard features. The purpose of these services is to help users better understand live event crew expectations, safety-awareness principles, communication habits, basic production terminology, and the professional behaviors expected on production worksites.

The Crew Blueprint does not provide guaranteed employment, staffing placement, union membership, employer authorization, legal advice, OSHA certification, equipment certification, rigging certification, forklift certification, electrical qualification, or permission to perform specialized work. Users must follow employer instructions, site-specific procedures, applicable law, and the direction of qualified supervisors on actual jobsites.

Scope and Application

This Privacy Policy applies to visitors, registered users, students, customers, contacts, and anyone else who interacts with The Crew Blueprint through thecrewblueprint.com or related services. It describes what information we may collect, why we collect it, how we protect it, when we may share it, and what choices users may have.
