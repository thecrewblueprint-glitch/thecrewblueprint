Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

The Crew Blueprint is an educational brand operated by Deadhang Labor LLC. This policy explains the information used by thecrewblueprint.com and the account-based learning experience.

18+ account rule: public sample pages may be viewed without an account. Creating an account requires a date of birth showing that the user is at least 18 years old. A date of birth showing the user is under 18 blocks account creation. Public browsing and ordinary sign-in to an existing eligible account do not require entering a birthday.

Information available without an account

Visitors may view public landing pages, course previews, Field Skills previews, Context Labs previews, legal pages, and other public information without creating an account. Normal web hosting may process technical request information such as IP address, browser/device information, requested URL, and timestamps for security and delivery. For a signed-out visitor with no existing Clerk session, the production design does not initialize Clerk merely to display public sample content; Clerk is initialized when authentication is requested or an existing session must be restored.

Account and authentication information

When you create or use an account, authentication is provided through Clerk and any social sign-in provider you choose, such as LinkedIn when that option is enabled. Depending on the method you choose, account information may include:

  • email address and account identifier;
  • name, profile image, or other profile information returned by an enabled social provider;
  • date of birth during account creation, used only to determine account eligibility; the full date of birth is not retained as part of the learner account after the eligibility check;
  • authentication-provider identifiers and session information;
  • account security events and technical information needed to operate authentication;
  • account and security records needed to operate authentication and enforce eligibility.

The Crew Blueprint does not ask you to send us your social-provider password. Authentication credentials are handled by Clerk and the selected identity provider according to their own systems and policies.

Why date of birth is required

Date of birth is requested at account creation to enforce the site's adult-only account rule. Public browsing does not require a birthday, and an existing eligible account is not asked to re-enter a birthday merely to sign in. The submitted date is used only long enough to determine eligibility. The full date of birth is not retained as part of the learner account; the service retains the adult-eligibility result and related verification metadata instead. Users must provide accurate information and may not misrepresent age to bypass this restriction.

How information is used

  • provide and secure account access;
  • enforce the 18+ eligibility rule;
  • deliver full free-tier learning to authenticated eligible users;
  • respond to support or legal requests;
  • detect abuse, fraud, security incidents, or attempts to bypass access controls;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

Cookies, sessions, and browser storage

Current learner progress is browser-based. Course progress and acknowledgments are currently stored in the user's browser and are not represented as synced account progress. If account-synced progress is introduced later, this policy will be updated before that feature is activated.

Authentication requires strictly necessary session technology. Clerk may use cookies or session tokens to keep an eligible account signed in. The account-creation age screen may use a short-lived first-party session marker after an ineligible entry to prevent immediate resubmission in the same browser session; that marker does not contain the submitted birth date. The site may also use browser storage for interface preferences, course acknowledgments, and browser-based progress. The current release does not use advertising cookies or third-party behavioral advertising trackers. See the Cookies Notice.

Service providers and disclosures

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the site, including Clerk for authentication, the selected social identity provider when you choose social sign-in, WordPress/hosting infrastructure for the production website, and email providers when you contact us. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users or the service, or investigate misuse.

California online tracking disclosures

The current release does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information about a visitor's online activities over time and across third-party websites or online services for behavioral advertising, and it does not intentionally allow third-party behavioral advertising trackers to do so through this site. Because that form of tracking is not currently used, browser Do Not Track signals do not change site behavior for behavioral advertising. We also do not currently sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If those practices change, this policy and any legally required opt-out controls will be updated before or when the change becomes active.

Payments and paid access

No paid checkout, subscription, or pricing flow is active in this release. The site may describe a future paid learning tier, but those descriptions do not create a purchase or paid entitlement.

Retention and account deletion

Account information is retained for as long as reasonably needed to provide the account, maintain security and records, or satisfy legal obligations. You may request account or personal-data deletion by contacting us. Some records may be retained when required by law or reasonably necessary for security, dispute, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain information about personal data. Requests can be sent to the contact address below. We may need to verify identity before acting on an account-related request.

Adults only for accounts and full course access

The Crew Blueprint does not permit people under 18 to create learner accounts or access full course content. Public sample pages are designed to be viewable without an account. If we learn that an ineligible account was created using false or incorrect age information, we may restrict or close it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the service. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site's data practices change. Material changes will be posted with a revised date and, where appropriate, presented for renewed acknowledgment.
