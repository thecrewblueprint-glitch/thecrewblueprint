First Five Calls

The complete Crew Blueprint map is large because live production is large. Beginners should not try to learn everything at once. Use this sequence to build call rhythm first.

Before Call 1

Complete Stagehand Fundamentals sections on arrival/check-in, basic hazards, chain of command, PPE/clothing expectations, communication, and stop/ask behavior.

Call 1 — receive, do, report back

Focus on assignment confirmation, ordinary assigned work, route awareness, and reporting completion/problems instead of trying to look experienced.

Call 2 — cases, carts, released cable, work-area state

Add bounded Field Skills only where the employer/lead has assigned and released the task.

Calls 3–4 — learn production rhythm

Notice load-in/show/strike differences, department ownership, standby vs break, reassignment, release, and timekeeping/sign-out.

Call 5 — choose a branch

Pick one department you have actually observed—Lighting, Audio, Video, Staging/Scenic, Backline/Props/Wardrobe, shop/logistics, or another path—and begin its support-level courses.
