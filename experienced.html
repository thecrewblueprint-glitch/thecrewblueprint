Depth model

Experience changes routing, not the meaning of the credential.

Knowing more can let you enter deeper material sooner. It does not turn course completion into employer authorization or specialist qualification.

Use experience to skip repetition

Start at systems, documentation, coordination, or leadership when that is the question you actually need answered. Return to a foundation node when you find a gap.

Do not use experience to erase boundaries

Site rules, employer assignment, current equipment, responsible department leadership, applicable standards, and qualified-person requirements still control real work.
