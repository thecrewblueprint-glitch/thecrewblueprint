About

Built for people learning how live production work actually fits together.

The Crew Blueprint is a practical learning system for live-event workers. It starts with orientation, support-level department knowledge, bounded Field Skills, and situation-based Context Labs so learners can build useful mental models without confusing online education with jobsite authorization.

Why it exists

Preparation before specialization.

Live production is often learned under time pressure, around unfamiliar equipment, changing conditions, and experienced crews. The free learning system gives learners a structured way to understand the environment before deeper technical training.

Jobsite readiness

Communication, assignments, handoffs, equipment stewardship, chain of command, and knowing when to stop and ask.

Transferable systems thinking

Common source-path-control-destination relationships and department interfaces that help learners understand what they are looking at.

Clear responsibility boundaries

Education is separated from employer training, qualification, certification, appointment, and permission to perform controlled work.
