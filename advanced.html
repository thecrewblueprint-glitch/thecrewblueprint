Responsibility pathways

When the work becomes coordination.

As responsibility grows, the job changes. You are expected to own clearer decisions, better documentation, stronger troubleshooting handoffs, crew coordination, quality control, and communication with other departments and stakeholders—not merely to know more equipment.
