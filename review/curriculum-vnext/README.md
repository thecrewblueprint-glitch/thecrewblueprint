## Evidence model

The comparison build combines:

- Roadmapdev curriculum/client-discovery analysis;
- Crew Blueprint research and the 143-ID classification;
- Production Atlas-derived role/employer/industry context through the reconciled Crew Blueprint role-family map;
- current 2026 hiring evidence from live-event employers/venues;
- public worker-demand/community evidence for relevance and hidden-rule discovery;
- current authoritative regulatory sources for safety/legal boundaries.

See `SOURCE_LEDGER.md` for the complete logged source set and evidence-class rules.

## Safety / qualification design

The proposed beginner courses intentionally keep hazardous/specialist domains at recognition, role-boundary, and stop/escalate level. They do not provide operational procedures for energized electrical work, temporary-power tie-ins, rigging, structural acceptance, powered-equipment operation, work at height, lasers, pyro, or other specialist activities.

Regulatory examples are not presented as the complete legal rule for every site or state. Employer/site rules, applicable jurisdiction, manufacturer instructions, and the responsible qualified/competent person remain controlling.

## Review questions

When comparing a proposed course with the current version, evaluate:

1. Is this easier for a first-call / early-career learner to understand?
2. Does it solve a problem workers actually describe?
3. Does it teach behavior employers currently value?
4. Is anything important from the current/archived versions missing?
5. Is any part still too dense or too shallow?
6. Does the specialist boundary feel clear without making the learner afraid to be useful?
7. Are sources available when needed without overwhelming the main lesson?
8. Which current or historical content blocks should be pulled into the proposed version verbatim or adapted?

## Canonicality

The canonical public curriculum remains whatever `main` identifies as current under the normal Crew Blueprint governance. This review surface is a decision aid until the owner explicitly promotes selected content.
