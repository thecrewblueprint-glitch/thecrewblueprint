Evidence without source-domain bleed

How the curriculum is grounded.

The course architecture is informed by recurring competency needs identified in private research, but learner-facing claims are grounded in legitimate educational, safety, regulatory, manufacturer, and technical sources. Production Atlas employment data and Deadhang business intelligence are not curriculum evidence.

OSHA · Walking-working surfaces

29 CFR 1910.22 supports the course emphasis on orderly work areas, safe access/egress, hazard correction, and walking-working-surface conditions.

Open OSHA 1910.22 ↗

OSHA · Personal protective equipment

29 CFR 1910.132 addresses hazard assessment, PPE selection, training, limitations, care, and employer responsibilities. PPE is never presented here as task authorization.

Open OSHA 1910.132 ↗

OSHA · Materials handling and storage

29 CFR 1910.176 supports the emphasis on clear aisles, safe storage, housekeeping, and materials-flow awareness.

Open OSHA 1910.176 ↗

Manufacturer documentation

Equipment-specific instructions belong to the manufacturer and employer/site procedures. Crew Blueprint does not convert one product’s procedure into a universal industry rule.

Consensus standards

Applicable ANSI/ESTA and other recognized consensus standards may support course research where relevant. Copyrighted standards are referenced and interpreted within allowable limits rather than reproduced.

Practitioner knowledge

Field-practice guidance is labeled as instructional framing or practitioner knowledge when it is not a legal, regulatory, manufacturer, or consensus-standard requirement.

System boundaries

What does not enter the curriculum.

No Deadhang business intelligence

Pricing, margins, clients, vendors, procurement, insurance strategy, market strategy, sourcing methods, and operating intelligence stay outside the learner-content pipeline.

No Production Atlas hiring data

Employer, pay, vacancy, market, labor-route, worker-review, and current opportunity information stays in Production Atlas. Crew Blueprint may link to Atlas but does not teach from that dataset.

Roadmapdev is filtered by domain

Only explicitly educational/curriculum research may support Crew Blueprint. Mixed business/employment packages must be split or rejected before use.

Course architecture vs. course claims: private research can reveal that communication, equipment handling, documentation, troubleshooting, shop/logistics, and safety boundaries recur across many roles. That can justify teaching those competencies. It does not authorize copying employer-specific claims, hiring intelligence, or commercial research into learner content.
