⚠️ Important Qualifications to Know

These are areas where the course uses common examples or Crew Blueprint frameworks that you should understand come with variability across real-world calls.

Communication Phrases (Module 6)
The course gives examples like "Copy," "Standing by," or "Heard." These are common on some sets, but every venue and department may use different vocabulary. Always ask your lead what phrases they use, and follow the call's protocol, not the textbook.
First 30 Days Plan (Module 10)
The course presents a week-by-week development sequence. This is a Crew Blueprint suggested framework, not an industry-standard probation timeline or certification. Real onboarding varies by employer, venue, and your previous experience.
Show Blacks (Module 3)
Show blacks are low-visibility clothing worn during performances when crew may be seen during blackout scenes. They are NOT general PPE and are NOT necessarily required for load-in/load-out. Requirements depend on the show and venue — ask at check-in.
Load-Out Risk (Module 9)
The course notes that load-out often involves fatigue, low visibility, and time pressure. These are real risk factors. However, load-in can be equally hazardous if it involves overhead work, time pressure, or complex setups. Hazard depends on the call, not the phase.
Phone Use (Module 10)
The course emphasizes staying available and aware. The exact phone-use policy varies by employer/venue — some are strict, others are flexible. Always ask your lead what the call's policy is.
