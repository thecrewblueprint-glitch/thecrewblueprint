Ownership and Permission

Support work around backline, props, wardrobe, and show-critical items is less about deciding how “sensitive” something looks and more about knowing who owns the item, what state it must stay in, and what you were actually assigned to change.

Replace the Sensitivity Scale With Permission

There is no reliable category of gear that a beginner can assume is “freely movable.”

An ordinary-looking case may contain a preset-dependent item. A simple stand may have a marked position. A garment may be part of a tracked quick change. A prop may be show-critical. A performer-owned item may have handling expectations that are not obvious from appearance.

Permission question

What exactly was I assigned to move, where does it go, and what state must remain unchanged?

Four Things to Identify

QuestionWhy it matters
Who owns or controls this item?Department, artist, performer, vendor, venue, or production ownership changes who can direct changes.
What is the assigned action?“Move this case” is not permission to open it, repack it, adjust contents, or change settings.
What state must be preserved?Position, preset, labeling, contents, fit, packing order, condition, or other show-critical state may matter.
Who accepts the handoff?A named department or person closes the loop and prevents an item from becoming ownerless in the workflow.

Assigned Movement vs. Changing State

  • Assigned movement: move or stage the identified item to the destination given by the responsible department.
  • State change: opening, adjusting, repacking, resetting, fitting, relabeling, reordering, tuning, modifying, or choosing a new position requires explicit department authority.
  • Unclear ownership: stop and get the responsible department before moving or changing anything.
  • Visible problem: report the condition instead of trying to “fix” a show-critical item.

Preset and Performer-Owned Items

Treat marked, preset-dependent, custom, costume, prop, backline, and performer-owned items as controlled handoffs.

The beginner skill is not guessing how fragile or expensive something is. It is preserving the department's state and asking before the assignment expands. This avoids the false rule that low perceived sensitivity equals broad permission.

Knowledge Check

Is any class of backline/props/wardrobe equipment automatically “freely movable”?

No. Permission comes from the current assignment and responsible department, not from how ordinary the item appears.

If you are told to move a case, can you reorganize its contents?

Not unless the responsible department explicitly assigns that change.

What should you preserve during a handoff?

The state the department expects: identification, position, preset, contents, packing, condition, or other show-critical information.

Publication Boundary

This owner-review course teaches ownership awareness, assigned movement, state preservation, and handoff discipline. It does not authorize adjustment, repair, tuning, fitting, repacking, reset, modification, or independent decisions about show-critical or performer-owned items.
