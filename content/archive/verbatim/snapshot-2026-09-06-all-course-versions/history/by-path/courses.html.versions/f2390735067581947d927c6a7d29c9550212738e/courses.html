Department starter courses

These are the five departments that currently have a public Course 1 entry point. The catalog does not use a more advanced course as a substitute when an entry course does not exist.

Lighting

Learn the support role around fixtures, cable, truss, and a lighting crew’s workflow.

  • Course 1 · Assigned WorkSupporting a Lighting Hang

Video

Learn the handling and support boundaries around LED video work.

  • Course 1 · Assigned WorkSupporting an LED Video Wall Build

Audio

Learn the support role around moving, staging, and cabling audio gear.

  • Course 1 · Assigned WorkSupporting an Audio Load-In

Staging & Carpentry

Learn component recognition, staging, and the boundary between helping and structural responsibility.

  • Course 1 · Assigned WorkSupporting Staging & Carpentry

Backline, Props & Wardrobe

Learn custody, handling, and the line between moving an item and changing show-critical work.

  • Course 1 · Assigned WorkSupporting Backline, Props & Wardrobe
Course 1 is the public department entry point.What am I being asked to support, and where does my responsibility stop?

Deeper systems, design, infrastructure, career, lead, and supervisor material is reviewed separately and does not need to be advertised as the learner’s starting point.

The Crew Blueprint provides orientation, job-readiness, and safety-awareness training for live event workers.
