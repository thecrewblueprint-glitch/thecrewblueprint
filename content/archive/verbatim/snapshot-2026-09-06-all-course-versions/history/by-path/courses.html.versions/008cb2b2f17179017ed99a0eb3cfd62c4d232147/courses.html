Learning Pathway Layers

Each layer makes a different claim. Foundation builds shared knowledge. Field Skills add observed practice. Department Course 1 teaches assigned support work. Department Course 2 explains the system around that work. Lead, supervisor, infrastructure, and Course 3 material adds bounded decision responsibility. The responsible authority still decides what you may do on the call.

Current publication status: Every course is live for the owner's audit. Treat all material after Stagehand Fundamentals as a public review copy until the owner finishes the course-specific accuracy, source, question, visual, and field-boundary audit.

Course 1: Learn to Perform Assigned Work "What am I being asked to do, and how do I do my part correctly?"

Recognize the gear, understand the boundary, and do the assigned support work under the responsible lead's direction.

Course 2: Begin Understanding the System "What system am I working inside, and where does my work fit?"

Trace how the department's systems work, why the procedure matters, where your task fits, and when the problem belongs to the technician or lead.

Course 3: Design, Document, and Verify the System "What requirements shape the complete system, and how will qualified people prove it is ready?"

Build a documented architecture, define interfaces and acceptance evidence, isolate failure domains, and keep specialist authority visible.
