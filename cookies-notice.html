Cookies Notice

The Crew Blueprint uses cookies and similar technologies to improve site performance, understand visitor behavior, and support a smoother user experience.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. They help websites remember preferences, maintain sessions, and understand how visitors interact with pages and content.

How We Use Cookies

  • To help the site function properly and maintain basic usability.
  • To remember preferences and improve user experience where applicable.
  • To understand how visitors use the site so content and navigation can be improved.
  • To support analytics, performance monitoring, and technical troubleshooting.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control, block, or delete cookies through browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect how certain parts of the site function or display.

Third-Party Services

Some third-party services integrated into the site, such as analytics providers, embedded content, affiliate tools, or media platforms, may also use cookies or similar technologies subject to their own privacy policies.

Updates to This Notice

This Cookies Notice may be updated periodically to reflect changes in site functionality, technology, legal requirements, or operational practices.
