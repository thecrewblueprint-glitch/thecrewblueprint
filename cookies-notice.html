Cookies Notice

The Crew Blueprint uses browser local storage for one purpose: remembering that you've dismissed the cookie notice banner. No tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies are used.

Last updated: August 16, 2026

What We Actually Use

When you first visit the site, a small notice appears explaining this. Clicking "Got it" saves a flag in your browser's local storage (not a cookie sent to a server) so the notice doesn't reappear on your next visit. That's the only browser storage this site uses.

What We Don't Use

  • No analytics or performance-tracking cookies.
  • No advertising, retargeting, or affiliate-tracking cookies.
  • No third-party embedded content (video players, social widgets) that would set their own cookies.
  • No session or authentication cookies — the site has no accounts or login.

Hosting-Level Data

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs standard technical request data (like IP address and timestamps) as part of normal web hosting, the same as any host. This is not a cookie set by The Crew Blueprint, and we don't access or use that data ourselves. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Managing Local Storage

You can clear the cookie-notice flag at any time by clearing your browser's site data for thecrewblueprint.com, or by using your browser's privacy/incognito mode. The notice will simply reappear on your next visit.

If This Changes

If The Crew Blueprint adds analytics, advertising, or third-party embedded content in the future, this notice will be updated first to describe exactly what's added and why, before it goes live.
