Cookies Notice

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This notice explains the cookies, session technology, and browser storage used by The Crew Blueprint.

Strictly necessary authentication technology

When you create an account or sign in, Clerk provides authentication. Clerk may set or read strictly necessary cookies and session tokens so the site can determine whether you are signed in, maintain the authenticated session, and protect account access. These technologies are required for the account experience to work.

Age-eligibility information

A date of birth is requested when a person attempts to create a new account. It is used to determine account eligibility; public browsing and ordinary sign-in to an existing account do not require re-entering a birthday. The production design is intended to retain an adult-eligibility result rather than the full submitted birth date after the eligibility check. If an entered birthday is ineligible, a short-lived first-party session marker may be set so the same browser session cannot immediately retry the age screen; that marker does not contain the submitted birth date.

Browser storage used by the learning experience

The site may use local or session storage for items such as course acknowledgment versions, interface preferences, temporary age-gate state, and browser-based progress. When account-persisted progress is implemented, the privacy policy will describe the account-linked record separately.

Social sign-in

If you choose an enabled social provider such as LinkedIn, that provider may use its own cookies or authentication technology on its own domains during sign-in. Those provider technologies are governed by that provider's policies.

What we do not currently use

The current release does not intentionally use third-party behavioral advertising cookies, ad retargeting cookies, or a paid checkout cookie flow. If analytics or advertising technology is added later, this notice will be updated before or when that change is activated as required.

Managing cookies and storage

Browser settings can clear cookies and local storage, but deleting strictly necessary authentication state will sign you out and may reset browser-only progress or acknowledgments. Blocking required session technology may prevent account features from working.
