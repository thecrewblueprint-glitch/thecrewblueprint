Organizations Worth Knowing

This site covers the basics. These organizations go deeper — standards, training, research, and professional development for people who want to grow further into the industry.

ESTA Technical Standards Program

The organization behind the technical standards used across entertainment production — rigging, stage machinery, electrical systems, lighting, and temporary structures. Worth knowing once you start hearing standard numbers referenced on the job.

IATSE Training Trust Fund

Safety training curricula and worker education for entertainment workers, including OSHA 10/30 resources — useful if you're looking to formalize training beyond what an employer provides.

USITT

Technical theatre research, education, and conferences. A good place to look if you want to see where the technical side of this industry is headed, or connect with people building it.
