Downloadable Checklists

Show up missing one thing and it's the thing everyone notices. Run through these before your first call, and before every load-out after it.

First Load-In Checklist

Run through this the night before, not on the drive over.

  • Read your call sheet fully — crew call time, sign-in location, PC name and number.
  • Set more than one alarm. Being late to your first call follows you.
  • Lay out all-black clothing and closed-toe shoes.
  • Pack work gloves, a basic multitool, and a flashlight or headlamp.
  • Pack earplugs.
  • Charge your phone fully, and bring a portable charger if you have one.
  • Bring cash for parking, in case card isn't an option.
  • Know exactly where you're parking before you leave the house.
  • Eat something before you go — you may not get a break right away.
  • Save the PC's number and the emergency contact in your phone.

Load-Out Checklist

How you leave a call matters as much as how you show up to it.

  • Don't leave until you're actually told you're released.
  • Return any tools or gear you borrowed before you go.
  • Sign out on the sign-in sheet if that's how the call tracks hours.
  • Don't walk off with gear that isn't yours — check your bag before you leave.
  • Double check you're not leaving any of your own belongings behind.
  • Thank your PC or lead on the way out. It matters more than you'd think for getting called back.
