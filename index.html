Stagehand Fundamentals

A 10-module course that prepares new stagehands to understand the jobsite, work safely, follow direction, recognize boundaries, and build the professional habits that get you called back.

Entry Level Jobsite Readiness Safety Awareness
  • What stagehands actually do, and how load-in, show call, and load-out differ
  • Safety mindset, PPE, and what to bring on your first call
  • Venue awareness, department basics, and tool and gear handling
  • Crew communication, etiquette, and what makes a new hand valuable
  • Getting hired, getting called back, and growing from there

Safety and training disclaimer

The Crew Blueprint provides orientation, job-readiness, and safety-awareness training for live event workers. It does not replace employer-provided safety training, site-specific instruction, union training, equipment certification, OSHA 10/30 training, or hands-on authorization for specialized work.
