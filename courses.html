Stagehand Fundamentals

A beginner-friendly orientation and safety-awareness course for people preparing to work load-ins, show calls, and load-outs in arenas, stadiums, festivals, convention centers, theaters, and corporate events.

Entry Level Jobsite Readiness Safety Awareness Live Events
  • Understand what stagehands actually do.
  • Learn the difference between load-in, show call, and load-out.
  • Understand basic crew structure and who gives direction.
  • Build habits that make new workers safer and easier to work with.
  • Start building a path toward future department-specific skills.
