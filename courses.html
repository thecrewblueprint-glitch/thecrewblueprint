Stagehand Fundamentals

A beginner-friendly course that prepares new stagehands to understand the jobsite, work safely, follow direction, recognize boundaries, and build the professional habits that get you called back. For people preparing to work load-ins, show calls, and load-outs in arenas, stadiums, festivals, convention centers, theaters, and corporate events.

Entry Level Jobsite Readiness Safety Awareness Live Events
  • Understand what stagehands actually do, and how load-in, show call, and load-out differ.
  • Build a safety mindset, know your PPE, and show up prepared with the right gear.
  • Move through a venue safely and recognize the basics of every department.
  • Communicate like a professional and build the habits that get you called back.
  • Know how to get hired, build a reputation, and grow beyond entry-level work.

Learning Pathways

After Stagehand Fundamentals, choose a department pathway to develop deeper knowledge and hands-on capability.

Lighting

Truss, cable, fixtures, and the exact line between helping and adjusting.

  • Course 1 Supporting a Lighting Hang
  • Course 2 Lighting Production Flow

Video

Handling, locking, and casing gear that's easy to damage and expensive to replace.

  • Course 1 Supporting an LED Video Wall Build
  • Course 2 Large-Scale LED Video Systems

Audio

Moving, staging, and cabling a PA system without touching what's already dialed in.

  • Course 1 Supporting an Audio Load-In
  • Course 2 Live Sound Systems

Staging & Carpentry

Real build work under direction — and why an assembled deck isn't automatically a finished one.

  • Course 1 Supporting Staging & Carpentry
  • Course 2 Modular Deck Systems

Backline, Props & Wardrobe

Ownership sensitivity: moving an item is labor, changing it is department work.

  • Course 1 Supporting Backline, Props & Wardrobe
  • Course 2 Department Systems
Course 1: Learn to Perform Assigned Work "What am I being asked to do, and how do I do my part correctly?"

Department-specific task execution, equipment recognition, authorization boundaries. Course 1 teaches you to reliably execute assigned department work under direction.

Course 2: Begin Understanding the System "What system am I working inside, and where does my work fit?"

How the department's systems work, why procedures matter, how your work fits into the larger production. Course 2 develops systems-level understanding of your department.
