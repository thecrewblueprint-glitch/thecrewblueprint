## Versioning rule

Change the acknowledgment version only when the legal agreement, required confirmations, eligibility, release, or other material assent term changes. A material version change must require fresh acceptance.

Do not change a legal page's updated date or the acknowledgment version merely because someone reviewed the files, refreshed an audit, or confirmed that no content change was necessary. Record a no-change review in the legal task log instead.
