## Tier boundaries

### Foundation

Completion may mean the learner worked through shared knowledge and self-checks. It does not mean the learner demonstrated a physical skill, became a department technician, or received worksite authorization.

### Stagehand Field Skills

An online knowledge unit may prepare the learner for practice. The practical state must identify the actual equipment, the observer, the conditions, and the acceptance result. The technician, driver, qualified load lead, department lead, barricade lead, venue lead, or other designated authority retains final say over method and acceptance.

### Department Support — Course 1

Course 1 prepares a learner to help correctly with assigned work. It does not grant authority to adjust, configure, repair, energize, route, tension, level, sign off, or change show-critical equipment without direction.

### Department Systems — Course 2

Course 2 builds a mental model of the department. Systems understanding is not independent operating authority. The learner should be better able to trace a handoff, communicate an observation, and recognize when to stop and escalate.

### Advanced Responsibility

Advanced material must name its limits. It cannot substitute for a license, certification, employer qualification, union status, manufacturer training, supervised experience, or legal authority.

## What the current build changes

- Repackages Stagehand Fundamentals into four focused entry points without deleting or renumbering lessons.
- Corrects the live lesson count from 40 to 34.
- Labels all five Course 1 pathways as Department Support and all five Course 2 pathways as Department Systems.
- Places the five proposed Field Skills between Fundamentals and department specialization, but shows their practitioner-review gate instead of linking unfinished courses.
- Replaces “independent operation” implications with systems-understanding and lead-authority language.
- Makes source boundaries and the Fundamentals bibliography easier to reach.
- Adds 21 full review builds, 153 learner stops, and 105 coached questions from completed Packages 24–39 and 46–50.
- Adds a standalone build register with an exact course-to-packet, route, tier, and release-gate map.

## What the current build deliberately does not claim

- It does not label the new review pages as practitioner-approved or publicly ready.
- It does not claim that reading, a quiz, or a simulated action proves field competency or lead readiness.
- It does not provide novice physical rigging or live-power technical instruction.
- It does not create a credential, qualification, or worksite authorization state.
- It does not invent persistent completion records in a static page that currently records only the learner's position in the selected part.
