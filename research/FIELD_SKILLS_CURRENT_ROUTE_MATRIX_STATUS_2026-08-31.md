## Route-specific findings

### Road Case

The current route remains the strongest general physical Field Skill candidate because it is ordinary material handling and already carries a named-task/observed-practice boundary. It still needs competent practitioner review of the physical-task rubric and AI-generated visuals before stronger release.

### Over-Under Cable Coiling

The current course is now traceable at claim and quiz level. Exact QSC evidence supports over-under as a common technique for compatible flexible production/audio cables. Neutrik/Belden evidence supports preserving cable-family/manufacturer exceptions. Observed practice on one named cable remains separate from broad cable authority.

### Ratchet Straps

The existing course correctly states that the responsible lead/manufacturer controls the exact mechanism and securement context, but the current matrix does not identify one exact manufacturer/model for the practical shown. Therefore the physical practical remains blocked from stronger release. This course must not be treated as cargo-system design, lifting/rigging, fall-protection, towing/recovery, or personnel-restraint training.

### Flatbed Cargo Support

The useful transferable learner content is role/authority literacy: a helper can perform an assigned bounded support task, but does not become the driver, motor carrier, regulatory inspector, or load-acceptance authority. Public-road cargo responsibility stays behind `GATE-DRIVER/CARGO`.

### Barricade

Early-career stagehands may encounter barricade work, but the practical method depends on the exact product/system, event plan, site conditions and controlling authority. Public accessibility, crowd management, traffic control, egress and AHJ acceptance are not granted by the course.

### Cable Ramps / Protectors

The route can teach product recognition, assignment boundaries, route awareness and escalation. Exact installation/configuration and whether a route satisfies accessibility, egress, traffic or venue requirements remain product/site/authority specific.
