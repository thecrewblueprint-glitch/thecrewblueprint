# Expanded Curriculum Research Queue

**Prepared:** 2026-08-30  
**Triggered by:** Build of 21 review courses from Packages 24–39 and 46–50  
**Purpose:** Collect only the evidence still needed to move review builds toward release, field observation, or a defensible decision not to build further.

## Queue rule

Do not rerun the source work already completed in Packages 24–39 and 46–50. Each item below must close a named release gap:

- field authenticity;
- model or jurisdiction applicability;
- authority boundary;
- original visual evidence;
- assessment validity;
- learner comprehension or retention;
- portfolio acceptance;
- durable record state;
- source freshness.

The 21-course route and evidence map is in the [Expanded Curriculum Build Register](EXPANDED_CURRICULUM_BUILD_REGISTER_2026-08-30.md).

## Priority A — physical Field Skills release evidence

### ECQ-01 — Five-skill practitioner acceptance run

**Status:** queued — blocking Field Skills release  
**Outcome:** One signed accuracy and authority review for each of the five Field Skills.

**Review panels**

1. Over-under: working audio or department technician plus fiber/video specialist for exceptions.
2. Ratchet straps: production/truck lead plus driver or cargo-securement reviewer for any vehicle example.
3. Flatbed support: current commercial driver or qualified load lead plus motor-carrier procedure owner.
4. Barricade: barricade lead for the exact system plus venue/event-operations reviewer.
5. Cable protectors: department or venue route owner plus accessibility and traffic-plan reviewer.

**Research prompt**

> Review the named Crew Blueprint course against the actual equipment and current workplace procedure. Mark every step as accurate, incomplete, model-specific, employer-specific, or outside the learner’s authority. Identify the lead decision, worker action, visible acceptance condition, and stop-work condition. Reject any statement that could imply universal procedure, field qualification, or permission to override the responsible authority.

**Deliverables**

- reviewer role, experience basis, date, equipment/model, and procedure version;
- sentence- or lesson-level corrections;
- accepted observation checklist;
- unresolved variations;
- permitted course claim after review.

**Done when:** All five courses have a named reviewer, exact equipment context, resolved critical comments, and accepted practical observation criteria.

### ECQ-02 — Original Field Skills visual and video production

**Status:** queued — blocking high-confidence mobile instruction  
**Depends on:** ECQ-01  
**Outcome:** Original, captioned, model-visible demonstrations that match the accepted procedure.

**Required asset sets**

| Skill | Required visuals |
|---|---|
| Over-under | Over and under hand positions; mirrored left-hand option; relaxed versus forced loop; connector and tie control; reel/fiber exception |
| Ratchet straps | Labeled assembly; inspection rejects; thread direction; slack removal; safe hand and recoil zones; lock; controlled release |
| Flatbed support | Complete restraint system; helper safe zones; damaged components; edge protection; lead-controlled tension order; driver acceptance; controlled release |
| Barricade | Family comparison; exact model label; audience versus working side; hand zones; deployment; full connector engagement; conflict stop; strike |
| Cable protectors | Product-family comparison; compatible parts; channels and connector clearance; full lid closure; accessible transition; vehicle/pedestrian controls; recheck |

**Evidence and rights record**

- asset owner and license;
- equipment model and procedure version;
- reviewer and approval date;
- captions, transcript, alt text, and text fallback;
- claim or lesson supported;
- renewal trigger when the model or procedure changes.

**Done when:** Every safety-critical physical step has an original reviewed visual, and each lesson remains complete if video playback fails.

### ECQ-03 — Practical observation reliability pilot

**Status:** proposed — blocking any practical-skill badge  
**Depends on:** ECQ-01 and ECQ-02  
**Outcome:** Determine whether two qualified observers apply each checklist consistently.

**Method**

- Use at least 10 novice observations per skill when feasible.
- Have two reviewers independently score a subset.
- Record disagreement by checklist line.
- Revise language that depends on unspoken expert judgment.
- Preserve model, cable, route, vehicle, surface, and environmental context.

**Done when:** The checklist produces consistent accept/rework/stop decisions and reviewers agree on what the recorded practice state means.

## Priority B — lead and supervisor release evidence

### ECQ-04 — Department lead role and scenario review

**Status:** queued — blocking seven Lead Tier releases  
**Courses:** common lead, lighting, audio, video, staging/carpentry, rigging, electrics

**Research prompt**

> For the actual market and production scale you work in, review the course’s responsibility map, work packages, verification model, change scenarios, and final claim. Identify title variation, authority held elsewhere, tasks that require separate qualification, missing paperwork or checks, and any scenario whose correct answer changes by employer, venue, union agreement, manufacturer, or jurisdiction.

**Sampling target**

- one touring reviewer and one venue/local-production reviewer for the common framework;
- at least two current practitioners per department where practical;
- a qualified rigging reviewer for Rigging Lead;
- qualified entertainment-electrical review for Electrics Lead;
- one small-show and one large-show perspective where roles combine differently.

**Done when:** Each course has a defensible shared core, labeled variations, accepted scenarios, and a reviewer-approved terminal claim.

### ECQ-05 — Predictive hazard evolving-scenario validation

**Status:** queued — blocking Supervisor Tier release  
**Outcome:** Test whether supervisors forecast the new work state rather than repeat hazard vocabulary.

**Scenario families**

- overhead and floor work begin simultaneously;
- cable or barricade route changes;
- outdoor weather or ground condition changes;
- public access starts before production fully clears;
- crew capability, fatigue, visibility, or staffing changes;
- a near miss exposes a failed handoff between departments.

**Done when:** Reviewers agree that the assessment distinguishes current-hazard spotting from forward-looking exposure and control reasoning.

### ECQ-06 — Event-operations integrated simulation

**Status:** queued — blocking Supervisor Tier release  
**Outcome:** Validate the federated-authority model in one realistic event scenario.

**Simulation must require**

- multiple department status reports;
- one resource conflict;
- one venue or public-interface constraint;
- one technical decision that the learner must return to a specialist;
- one client or schedule change;
- one safety or weather change;
- an integrated ready, constrained, blocked, or held decision;
- written decision and closeout records.

**Done when:** Current event/venue and production supervisors accept both the decision route and grading rubric.

## Priority E — learning quality, records, and source lifecycle

### ECQ-15 — Knowledge-question validity and retention pilot

**Status:** queued — required before claiming strong retention  
**Scope:** 105 new questions plus sampled questions from existing Fundamentals and department courses.

**Method**

- novice comprehension test;
- expert answer-key review;
- immediate and delayed recall checks;
- distractor analysis;
- confidence rating;
- authority-boundary error analysis;
- revision of ambiguous, trivial, or cueing questions.

**Minimum report**

- sample and learner background;
- item difficulty and distractor behavior;
- delayed retention window;
- safety- or authority-critical misses;
- before/after question revisions;
- permitted learning-quality claim.

**Done when:** Evidence supports a measured statement about comprehension or retention. Do not use a synthetic “retention score” before this pilot.

### ECQ-16 — Learning and authority record model

**Status:** queued — product dependency  
**Outcome:** Define durable, auditable states without turning a static quiz into authorization.

**Required states**

- lesson progress;
- knowledge completed;
- practice scheduled;
- practice observed on named equipment;
- rework required;
- employer/lead authorization;
- external credential verified;
- expiration or review date;
- reviewer and evidence link.

**Done when:** The data model, access rules, audit trail, correction process, privacy rules, and user-facing language have owner approval.

### ECQ-17 — Source, model, and rights freshness registry

**Status:** queued — maintenance dependency  
**Outcome:** Know when a course needs re-review because a source, regulation, product, role description, or media right changed.

**Required fields**

- course, lesson, claim, and question IDs;
- source owner, title, URL, version or date, access date, and jurisdiction/model scope;
- evidence type: law, standard, manufacturer, employer/role, association, practitioner, or Crew Blueprint framing;
- visual/video owner, license, transcript, and fallback;
- review interval and change trigger;
- current reviewer and disposition.

**Done when:** Every safety- or authority-critical claim in the 21 builds has an owner, source, applicability note, and next-review trigger.
