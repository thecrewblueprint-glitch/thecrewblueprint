# The Crew Blueprint — Ground / Field-Hand Foundation Architecture

**Date:** 2026-09-06  
**Status:** proposed canonical foundation for owner approval; not yet accepted publication architecture

## Decision

The foundational learner system should be treated as **one foundation course plus one practical Field Skills library**, not as dozens of equal courses.

### 1. Stagehand Fundamentals — foundation course

Primary job: reduce first-call uncertainty and teach the operating system of a production call.

Core content to keep or consolidate here:

- reading call information, call sheets, stage plots, setup diagrams, and venue instructions at an entry level;
- arriving prepared and communicating availability/delays appropriately;
- who gives direction and how assignments move through leads, technicians, stage management, and production leadership;
- **Listen → Clarify → Report Back**;
- situational awareness and stop/ask behavior;
- department overview and role/authority boundaries;
- equipment stewardship and **Preserve → Verify → Report**;
- basic load-in / changeover / load-out / reset context;
- reliability, staying findable, follow-through, discretion, and reputation;
- first-five-calls orientation and realistic next-learning choices;
- short introduction to the employer ecosystem without pretending there is one universal ladder.

Existing registered identities that should be absorbed into Fundamentals rather than remain separate courses include `C-COM-WORK`, `C-OUT-FIELD`, and `C-CAR-FIRST-FIVE`.

## Field Skill design rule

A Field Skill should answer a narrow learner problem that can be understood safely without implying independent authority. It should contain:

1. what the task/support context is;
2. what the learner should notice;
3. what clear communication/handoff looks like;
4. what varies by site, employer, product, or responsible lead;
5. when to stop and ask;
6. what the learner is **not** being qualified or authorized to do;
7. source/evidence lineage appropriate to the claim;
8. owner/practitioner review state.

## Road Case status

`C-FLD-ROAD-CASE` remains **not closed as an owner-review task**. Existing research and the route remain reusable, but the requested owner field-context interview/rebuild cannot be declared complete until the owner supplies or confirms the intended field observations. Do not invent those observations.

## Product rule

The customer should see:

**Stagehand Fundamentals → Field Skills Library → Department Starters**

The internal registry may keep many more identities for evidence, history, specialist awareness, and future modules, but those identities must not dictate the public product count.
