## Initial learner-facing career lanes

The first version of the hybrid front door should expose six independent lane entries:

1. **Stagehand / General Production Labor** — `C-SHF-FUNDAMENTALS`
2. **Lighting** — `C-LTG-SUPPORT`
3. **Audio** — `C-AUD-SUPPORT`
4. **Video / LED** — `C-VID-SUPPORT`
5. **Rigging** — `C-RIG-AWARE`, explicitly as bounded awareness/entry rather than practical qualification
6. **Staging / Structures** — `C-STG-SUPPORT`

Stagehand is therefore a major generalist lane, **not a universal prerequisite** for the other five.

## Common foundation

`C-COM-WORK` is mapped to a shared Common Foundation layer because communication, direction, clarification, reporting back, and handoff discipline apply across career lanes.

The shared layer should remain a reusable graph concept rather than becoming another large mandatory course. Other universally useful concepts can be linked or extracted from lane material later without changing the primary lineage assignment of those course IDs.

## Stagehand and Field Skills

Stagehand Fundamentals remains the Stagehand/general-production lane entry.

The **18 Field Skill identities remain intact but present as one Field Skills Library**, not eighteen equal main courses. `C-LOG-PRODUCTION` becomes a Stagehand lane context module and `C-CAR-FIRST-FIVE` becomes a situation-first entry module within the Stagehand start path.

## Department lane structure

Lighting, Audio, Video/LED, and Staging/Structures each resolve into the same general product pattern:

**lane entry → sequenced core modules → deep/advanced material**

This removes the catalog problem in which container courses and detail courses compete as peers.

Rigging follows a stricter pattern:

**bounded awareness entry → gated systems literacy → deep owner/practitioner-reviewed advanced material**

No part of the map converts online learning into rigging qualification, work-at-height authorization, load-calculation authority, or site appointment.

## Future lanes already present inside V2

Thirty-six IDs already support future career lanes. The strongest near-term candidates are:

- Production Power / Electrical
- Backline / Props / Wardrobe, with deeper discipline split
- Logistics / Warehouse / Shop
- Stage Management
- Production Management / Production Office
- Venue / Site Operations
- broader Production / Coordination bridge material

These are retained in the graph but can remain hidden from the initial learner front door until their lane architecture is ready.

## Material that should move out of the main course catalog

Career-entry, professional-practice, union/CBA awareness, credentials, freelance-business, rates/scope/payment, work-records, and worker-rights identities remain valuable. They belong primarily under **Find Work**, **Grow**, or a worker business/rights resource hub rather than competing with technical training courses.

Sector-specific identities — touring/festival, venue/house, corporate/convention, theatre, worship, broadcast, and outdoor/weather — should become **Context Labs/reference material** so learners see how principles transfer between work environments without creating seven more top-level course routes.

## Specialist awareness

Electrical interfaces, RF, automation, atmospheric effects, lasers, pyro/flame/cryogenic effects, and production-network cyber hygiene remain deep/context-triggered specialist-awareness material. They should appear when relevant to a learner's lane or environment, not as ordinary beginner permissions.

## Overlap / merge findings

Nine important overlap clusters should drive the next architecture pass:

1. **Lighting core:** `C-LTG-PRODUCTION-FLOW` plus lifecycle/science/control/console/design-documentation identities should become one sequenced Lighting core with retained module IDs.
2. **Audio core:** `C-AUD-SYSTEMS` plus signal/sources/console/PA/network/intercom/sector identities should become one sequenced Audio core.
3. **Video core:** `C-VID-SYSTEMS` plus LED hardware/signal/switching/projection/camera/media/AoIP identities should become one Video systems pathway, while retaining the option for a later broadcast/camera split.
4. **Staging core:** deck, systems, shop, platform, movement, and lifecycle identities should become one Staging/Structures sequence rather than overlapping peer courses.
5. **Backline / Props / Wardrobe:** keep a shared gateway, then split deeper learning into three sublanes.
6. **Automation:** `C-AUT-STAGE` and `C-AUT-AWARE` should present as one bounded awareness resource unless later evidence establishes a meaningful learner distinction; both IDs and historical bodies remain preserved.
7. **Event operations:** the older integrated `C-SUP-EVENT-OPS` should be decomposed across Stage Management, Production Management, and Venue/Site Operations rather than compete with those clearer lanes.
8. **Outdoor/festival context:** touring, outdoor-sector, field-operations, and heat/changing-conditions material should route through Context Labs to prevent repeated environmental framing.
9. **Universal career ladder:** `C-CAR-LADDERS` is the only identity whose current premise should be retired. Accurate material about lateral movement and role combinations should be salvaged into the branching career map.

## Corpus disposition

**142 of 143 identities retain active value as learning, reference, specialist-awareness, cross-lane, career-resource, or future-lane material.**

The remaining identity, `C-CAR-LADDERS`, still retains historical/source value; only its universal-ladder framing is obsolete. Archive retention remains in force for all 143 identities and every materially distinct historical body.

## Product interpretation

The correct product is not a 143-course catalog and not a clean-sheet product that discards most of V2.

It is:

> **A clean, career-guided learner shell over the full evidence-backed V2 graph.**

The learner should see a small number of meaningful decisions. The system should retain the larger graph underneath and surface deeper material only when the learner's lane, context, career question, or specialist boundary makes it relevant.

## Next architecture operation

Once this mapping is accepted, the next structural pass should convert the primary mapping into explicit learner-path edges:

`entry → common/shared nodes → lane core → contextual branches → advanced/deep nodes → Find Work / Grow bridges`

That pass should define prerequisites/recommendations, optional branches, context triggers, and Production Atlas career-intelligence bridge points without rewriting the underlying curriculum yet.
