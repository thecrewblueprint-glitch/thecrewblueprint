## What was built

Completed research Packages 24–39 and 46–50 now map to **21 learner-facing review builds**:

- 5 Stagehand Field Skills courses;
- 7 Lead courses;
- 2 Supervisor courses;
- 5 Department Course 3 system/design courses;
- 1 Production Infrastructure awareness course;
- 1 Production & Coordination career-branch course.

Together, the pages contain:

- **153 learner stops**, including the assessment and evidence stop on each page;
- **105 multiple-choice scenario questions**, with three choices, one keyed answer, and coaching feedback for every question;
- approximately **21,200 learner-facing words** across lessons, assessments, and practice gates;
- a source-lineage section, authority boundary, and practice or qualified-review gate on every course.

The new pages use the same dark, gold, fixed-progress, desktop-sidebar, mobile-selector, card, quiz, and previous/next design language as Stagehand Fundamentals and the department courses.

## Claim states

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Review build | The research supports a structured course draft and assessment. The page is available for owner and practitioner review on the branch. |
| Knowledge completed | The learner finished the reading and knowledge check. This is not evidence of physical performance. |
| Practice observed | A named reviewer observed a named task on identified equipment under recorded conditions. |
| Qualified review | A practitioner with the actual discipline responsibility reviewed system, authority, and assessment accuracy. |
| Employer authorization | The employer or other controlling organization permits the person to perform named work. Crew Blueprint does not grant this state. |
| External credential or legal qualification | A credentialing, licensing, regulatory, union, manufacturer, or other external authority controls this state. Crew Blueprint does not grant it. |

## What the current evidence does not support yet

The completed packets support the course structures and bounded lesson content above. They do **not** yet support these additional claims or builds:

1. Publicly labeling any new page as practitioner-approved or jobsite-ready.
2. Original model-specific diagrams or demonstration video for the five physical skills.
3. A generic physical rigging course, rigging practical, or overhead-work signoff.
4. A self-study portable-power technical course with connection, testing, energization, repair, or live-fault procedures.
5. Product-menu instruction presented as transferable systems mastery.
6. A Backline/Props/Wardrobe Course 3; the current corpus does not contain a Course 3 packet for that combined pathway.
7. Evidence that the 105 new knowledge questions meet a target retention or discrimination score with real learners.
8. A durable LMS record for knowledge completion, observed practice, employer authorization, credentials, or renewal dates.

Those gaps now have targeted work items in [RESEARCH_QUEUE.md](RESEARCH_QUEUE.md). They do not require rerunning source work already completed in Packages 24–39 and 46–50.

### Rating

- **Curriculum material as a review build: 8.4 / 10.** The set is substantive, tiered, source-mapped, scenario-assessed, and unusually careful about authority boundaries. Practitioner corrections, deeper Course 3 portfolio cases, and some repeated Lead-tier assessment patterns keep it below release quality.
- **Public-release readiness: 5.8 / 10.** The pages work as review builds, but original reviewed visuals, field observation reliability, qualified department review, learner testing, source-freshness records, durable learner states, and a real desktop/mobile browser pass remain open.
- **Measured learning-retention rating: not yet available.** Assigning a high score before ECQ-15 would manufacture evidence that the project does not have.

The release decision should remain **review only / noindex** until the course-specific gates in this register close.

## Build method and maintenance

- `scripts/course-data-field.mjs` is the source record for the five Field Skills builds.
- `scripts/course-data-lead.mjs` is the source record for the nine Lead/Supervisor builds.
- `scripts/course-data-advanced.mjs` is the source record for the seven advanced and cross-functional builds.
- `scripts/build-tiered-courses.mjs` writes the 21 standalone HTML pages.
- `scripts/validate-tiered-courses.mjs` checks course structure, quiz keys, answer-position balance, source-packet lineage, generated files, and live internal links.
- `css/tiered-course.css` and `js/tiered-course.js` provide the shared course player.
- Generated HTML carries its full course record in the page. A generated page is not an empty redirect or a placeholder.

After changing curriculum data, run:

```bash
node scripts/build-tiered-courses.mjs
node scripts/validate-tiered-courses.mjs
```

Then complete the browser check before committing the generated pages.
