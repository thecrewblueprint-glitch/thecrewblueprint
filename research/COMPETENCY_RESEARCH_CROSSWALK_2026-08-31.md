| State | Meaning |
|---|---|

# 12. Main evidence gaps after broad research

The dominant remaining gaps are no longer unknown subject areas. They are **validation and exactness gaps**:

1. claim-level source normalization and support edges;
2. practitioner acceptance of physical/system/lead material;
3. exact manufacturer/model and jurisdiction applicability;
4. original reviewed visual/video evidence;
5. novice comprehension/usability;
6. delayed retention and scenario transfer;
7. observed-practice reliability;
8. direct employer/buyer/customer evidence;
9. willingness-to-pay/procurement/business economics;
10. content production/maintenance capacity;
11. privacy/security/backend workflow validation;
12. source freshness/version lifecycle.

**Research-universe status:** broad curriculum domain discovery is substantially complete. Move primary effort to competency/content crosswalk, matrix backfill, targeted gap closure and validation rather than creating new generic research queues.
