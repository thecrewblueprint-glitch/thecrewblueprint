# Source Prompt

Act as an expert stagehand mentor and production operations consultant. Create a comprehensive research packet designed for someone entering the professional stagehand industry for their first two years.

Your objective is to provide a structured, high-level overview of the life and career progression of a professional stagehand. Please organize the packet into the following dimensions:

- **Workflow Dynamics:** Explain the typical lifecycle of a show (load-in, show call, load-out) and the professional standards of conduct expected during each phase.
- **Core Competency Progression:** Define a 'skill acquisition roadmap' for the first 24 months, separating essential 'day-one' knowledge from the advanced technical skills that should be prioritized by the end of the second year.
- **Risk & Safety Framework:** Detail the non-negotiable safety standards (e.g., situational awareness, PPE, hazard recognition) and distinguish between general safety culture and tasks requiring formal certifications or site-specific authorization.
- **Industry Literacy:** Identify the essential 'unspoken rules' of the jobsite, common pitfalls for beginners, and the professional terminology a newcomer must master to be effective.

## Output Requirements

- **Structured Analysis:** Use tables or bulleted sections for clarity.
- **Critical Evaluation:** Include a section on 'Underestimated Risks' and 'Blind Spots' for new workers.
- **Research Roadmap:** Suggest specific areas, industry standards (e.g., OSHA, ETCP, ESTA), or technical topics the user should investigate next to deepen their expertise.
- **Uncertainty/Caveats:** Explicitly note where practices vary based on region, venue type, or union vs. non-union environments.

## Optional Add-Ons

- Create a 'First 90 Days' checklist of items to learn, tasks to master, and questions to ask a crew lead.
- Compare the professional expectations of a house stagehand versus a touring stagehand.
- Challenge my assumptions: What are the most common misconceptions people have about working in live event production?
- Turn this into a research plan: Which specific technical manuals, safety standards, or organizations should I prioritize reading first?
- Create a 'Decision Framework' for handling high-pressure or potentially unsafe situations on a busy load-in.

## Research Directions

- **Industry Standards:** ESTA (Entertainment Services and Technology Association) and ANSI standards, OSHA (Occupational Safety and Health Administration) event production guidelines.
- **Certifications:** ETCP (Entertainment Technician Certification Program) pathways and the role of specialized training (e.g., forklift, aerial lift, rope access).
- **Logistics & Workflow:** Understanding the 'Chain of Command' on a professional production site (Production Manager vs. Stage Manager vs. Head Carpenter/Electrician).
- **Professional Development:** The role of IATSE (International Alliance of Theatrical Stage Employees) and how union vs. freelance employment models differ in terms of training and job security.
- **Physical & Mental Health:** Ergonomics, hearing protection, and the realities of irregular, high-intensity work hours.
