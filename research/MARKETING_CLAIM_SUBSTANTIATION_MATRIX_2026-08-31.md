# Marketing Claim Substantiation Matrix

**Prepared:** 2026-08-31  
**Purpose:** Prevent curriculum maturity, research volume, or testimonials from turning into unsupported marketing claims.

| Claim family | Default status | Evidence required before use | Safe current alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Industry-approved" | Prohibited unless specifically documented | Named approving organizations + scope + written permission | "Research-backed live-event education" |
| "Certified" / "certification" | Prohibited for ordinary Crew Blueprint completion | Valid credential program with published requirements and governance | "Course completion" / "knowledge check" |
| "OSHA certified" | Prohibited unless actual authorized program applies | OSHA-authorized training evidence | "Does not replace OSHA training" |
| "Gets you hired" | Unsupported | Controlled/credible outcome data | "Designed to improve job-context readiness" |
| "More callbacks" | Unsupported | Longitudinal learner/employer outcome evidence | "Focuses on reliability, communication and job readiness" |
| "Reduces incidents" | Unsupported | Defined incident metric + comparative evidence | "Teaches hazard recognition and stop/escalate boundaries" |
| "Employers prefer" | Unsupported | Representative employer research with methodology | "Employer/buyer research is ongoing" |
| "Qualified to perform" | Prohibited unless external authority says so | Actual employer/legal/certification authority | "Knowledge does not create jobsite authorization" |
| "Expert reviewed" | Prohibited until recorded | Named reviewer, domain, date, scope, unresolved notes | "Research foundation" |
| "ADA compliant" | Avoid blanket claim | Accessibility audit with scope/date + legal review as appropriate | "Accessibility is a release requirement; issues can be reported" |

## Testimonials and endorsements

Before publishing a testimonial or endorsement:
- document permission;
- document any material relationship;
- do not fabricate or materially edit meaning;
- do not present atypical outcomes as guaranteed;
- keep any required disclosure clear and near the endorsement/recommendation.

FTC starting points:
- https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/endorsements-influencers-reviews
- https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking

## Operating rule

Marketing claims should have the same architecture as curriculum claims: **claim → evidence → scope → date/version → owner → review state**.
