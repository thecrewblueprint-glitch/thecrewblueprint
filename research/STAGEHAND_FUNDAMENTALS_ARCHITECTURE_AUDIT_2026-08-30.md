## Duplication, gap, and prerequisite report

### Preserved reference material

- The single-page course and the archived standalone module pages are delivery/reference variants of the same foundation material. They are not separate lessons and must not be added together in completion counts.
- The visible course bibliography remains the source boundary record for learner-facing claims. The course map and first lesson of each part now link to it.

### Content that remains introductory

- Module 8 introduces general gear-handling judgment. It does not establish field competency in over-under coiling, ratchet straps, flatbed securement support, barricade assembly, or cable-ramp placement.
- Those five tasks belong in the Stagehand Field Skills layer after Fundamentals. Packages 46–50 are source packets under practitioner review, not authorization to publish skill instruction.

### Claim corrections carried into the proposed build

- Show blacks are show- or employer-specific clothing direction, not PPE by default.
- Call-and-response phrases are examples of clear crew communication, not universal commands.
- Early-call expectations and beginner assignments vary by employer, local, venue, and production.
- The early-calls action plan is Crew Blueprint instructional framing, not a national onboarding standard.
- Specialization may lead to employer training, supervised practice, or an external credential where applicable; the course does not imply that a credential automatically follows.

## Assessment blueprint

### Retained now

- Keep the existing lesson-level knowledge checks and coaching explanations.
- Treat them as self-checks for recognition and judgment, not practical-performance verification.

### Proposed after review

- Add one short part-end knowledge check after each of the four parts.
- Add an umbrella Fundamentals final only after the owner approves the blueprint and a novice learner test confirms the language and length.
- If a randomized final is adopted, define and review the question bank before claiming a 20-question assessment. No such final is complete in the current build.

## Learner-state model

The product must keep these states separate:

1. **Lesson position recorded** — the learner knows where they stopped in a part.
2. **Lesson knowledge check completed** — a self-check was answered.
3. **Part knowledge completed** — a future part-end check was completed.
4. **Fundamentals knowledge completed** — a future umbrella knowledge standard was met.
5. **Field practice observed** — a named practical task was witnessed on the real equipment selected by the responsible lead.
6. **Employer or lead authorization granted** — the worksite authority permitted the learner to perform a task in that context.
7. **External credential verified** — a separate issuing body confirmed a license, certification, card, or qualification where one applies.

No earlier state implies a later one. The current split-course interface reports position within the selected part only; it does not claim durable completion tracking.
