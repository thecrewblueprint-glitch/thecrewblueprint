## Project/business research applicability

Public practitioner evidence should also be added where it can reduce unsupported product/business assumptions:

- what workers actually want before a first call;
- what employers/leads wish new hands understood;
- common training frustrations and gaps;
- how shops/vendors develop technicians;
- how credentials/course completion are perceived in hiring;
- what progress/history/evidence workers would actually find useful;
- what supervisors would trust from a training platform versus what they would still verify themselves.

These are **not yet treated as validated customer demand or willingness-to-pay**. They become inputs to PROJECT-E01 customer/learner discovery, not substitutes for interviews/tests.
