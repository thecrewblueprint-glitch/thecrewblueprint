## Current audience-needs themes for owner audit

The current discovery evidence repeatedly surfaces:

- first-call uncertainty and hidden jobsite expectations;
- reliability, attendance, reading call information, and follow-through;
- listening and asking for clarification instead of guessing;
- closed-loop communication and staying findable;
- career-path uncertainty;
- networking / referrals / professional trust;
- department selection and progression;
- work availability, seasonality, and irregular income;
- touring/festival travel and sustainability realities;
- role/ownership boundaries and preserving other people's systems;
- professional culture, respect, discretion, and mentorship.

These themes are **needs evidence**, not technical/safety authority.

## Audit rule

For each active course/lesson, owner review should classify content as one of:

- Keep — audience-critical
- Keep — factual foundation
- Simplify / humanize
- Move earlier
- Move later / advanced
- Move to career/resource layer
- Needs community validation
- Needs practitioner validation
- Needs authoritative technical/legal source
- Remove / duplicate / not solving a target need
