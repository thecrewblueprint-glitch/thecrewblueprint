## 4. Learner/customer evidence register

### LRN-001 — Learner segmentation

**State:** identified/partial  
**Need evidence from:** beginners, 1–6 month workers, recurring general hands, department learners, technicians, leads, career changers, venue/shop workers.

Questions:

- What are they trying to achieve?
- What scares/confuses them?
- What do they search for before/after calls?
- What prior knowledge do they actually have?
- What prevents learning completion?
- What format do they use on mobile/jobsite?

### LRN-002 — First-call journey study

**State:** identified  
Observe/interview around preparation → arrival → assignment → work → downtime → release.

### LRN-003 — Information-architecture study

**State:** deferred until more content evidence exists  
Compare linear curriculum, task lookup, department pathway, competency map and hybrid structures.

### LRN-004 — Visual learning study

**State:** identified/partial  
Test labeled diagrams, sequence panels, photos, video, animation, signal-flow diagrams and good/bad examples.

### LRN-005 — Mobile/jobsite use

**State:** identified  
Research one-handed use, poor connectivity, glare/night conditions, quick-reference behavior and acceptable lesson length.

### LRN-006 — Assessment/retention/transfer

**State:** partially defined in ECQ  
Need real learner data: comprehension, delayed retention, scenario transfer and observed performance.

### LRN-007 — Trust/claim interpretation

**State:** identified  
Test whether users misread completion badges/statuses as certification, qualification or employer permission.

## 14. Architecture decision order

Once evidence is mature enough, decide in this order:

1. competency/content dependency architecture;
2. learner information architecture and course format;
3. customer/product scope;
4. learning/practice state model;
5. backend roles/data/content/versioning architecture;
6. analytics/privacy/security architecture;
7. testing/beta process;
8. release cadence/calendar.

This order prevents the backend from hard-coding a curriculum structure that later evidence disproves.

## 15. Final principle

The repository should accumulate **research ahead of publication**.

The desired operating condition is:

> a deep backlog of researched, sourced, reviewable content and product requirements exists behind the public release line, allowing deliberate scheduled updates while infrastructure and testing continue to improve.

That is the evidence posture this register controls.
