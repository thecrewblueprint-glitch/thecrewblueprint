It must not override controlling regulation, exact manufacturer instructions, employer/venue procedure, engineering decisions, or specialist qualification requirements.

When real crews vary, teach the transferable principle and explicitly preserve the current-system/current-crew verification step.

## Controlled-work boundary

This structural pass may map recognition, systems reasoning, authority boundaries, stop/escalate rules, and evidence gaps. It does **not** create or expand operational instruction for:

- energized temporary power;
- overhead rigging, hoist, or fly operation;
- structural acceptance, loading, or engineering decisions;
- suspended-system rigging;
- hazardous/specialist prop or effect work;
- product/model procedures without the exact manufacturer/system context and appropriate review.
