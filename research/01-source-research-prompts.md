# Source Research Prompts — Stagehand Fundamentals Course 1 Gaps

This template collects Perplexity search prompts designed to find authoritative sources for the 10 research gaps identified in the Stagehand Fundamentals evaluation (2026-08-21).

**Purpose:** Harden the evidence underneath Course 1 by converting unsourced "industry common knowledge" into either sourced claims or clearly labeled practitioner guidance.

**Evaluation baseline:** Current research foundation rates 5.5–6/10 for traceability/source defensibility. These searches are designed to move gaps from "informed but unsourced" to "sourced" or "clearly labeled practitioner guidance."

**Reconciliation (2026-08-30):** Prompts 1–10 are complete and indexed in [02-source-master-map.md](02-source-master-map.md), with later validation in Packages 15–20A and the Stagehand Fundamentals authenticity audit. Do not rerun them by default. The bonus assessment-design question is carried forward to [RQ-100](RESEARCH_QUEUE.md#rq-100--stagehand-fundamentals-architecture-audit).

**How to use:**
1. Copy each prompt verbatim into Perplexity
2. Ask it to return results as a list: link + source type (regulation/standard/manufacturer/union/publication)
3. Log the results in the "Sources Found" column below
4. Prioritize primary sources (OSHA CFR, ANSI standards, union docs, manufacturer specs) over news/secondary coverage
5. Flag any results that contradict existing Course 1 material for owner review
