## Prompt and production record

All five assets were generated as original, wide instructional sequences for the course pages. Prompts required:

- realistic live-event equipment and work settings;
- visible hand, foot, pinch-zone, recoil-zone, route, or safe-zone controls appropriate to the named task;
- one consistent equipment family within a sequence;
- no branding or copied manufacturer artwork;
- no claim that the picture is the exact procedure for unverified equipment;
- dark industrial colors with safety-gold accents consistent with The Crew Blueprint.

The images were converted to optimized JPEG files at a maximum 1600-pixel dimension for practical mobile delivery. Alt text and learner-facing captions were added in the course source rather than embedded into the bitmap.
