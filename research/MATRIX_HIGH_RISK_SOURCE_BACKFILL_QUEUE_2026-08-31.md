# The Crew Blueprint — High-Risk Source Backfill Queue

**Prepared:** 2026-08-31  
**Status:** Active matrix priority queue  
**Purpose:** Backfill exact claim-level evidence in risk order before spending equivalent effort on low-risk descriptive copy.

This queue is about **source traceability and authority boundaries**, not operational instruction. Controlled work remains outside Crew Blueprint qualification authority.

---

# Priority model

Rank a claim family higher when an error could materially affect:

1. physical safety;
2. legal/regulatory interpretation;
3. specialist qualification/authorization;
4. structural/electrical/rigging responsibility;
5. public egress/accessibility;
6. employer/venue acceptance;
7. assessment validity or misleading credential interpretation.

Use exact regulations, standards, credential/job analyses, manufacturer documents, venue/AHJ material and practitioner review. Organization home pages are discovery links only.

- `pathway-electrics-03-system-load-planning.html`
- `lead-electrics.html`
- Lighting/Video/Staging courses that reference temporary power interfaces

**Exact evidence classes needed**

- applicable OSHA electrical regulations/guidance;
- current NFPA/NEC references and local/AHJ adoption context where a claim depends on code;
- ETCP electrical/PPDT role and eligibility/job-analysis evidence;
- exact manufacturer documentation for product/system-specific claims;

**Matrix rule**

Every learner-facing statement about who may connect, test, energize, configure, inspect or accept a system must have an explicit authority owner and applicability qualifier.

**Disposition:** `P0 SOURCE + QUALIFICATION REVIEW`

- applicable OSHA fall-protection/work-at-height rules where claimed;

# Completion condition

This queue is closed only when each affected high-risk learner-facing claim has one of these explicit dispositions:

- `verified_current`;
- `verified_with_qualification`;
- `crew_blueprint_framework_clear`;
- `needs_practitioner`;
- `needs_model_or_jurisdiction_scope`;
- `revise_content`;
- `remove_or_replace`;
- `superseded`.

No high-risk claim should remain supported only by an organization home page, a generic bibliography entry, or an unlabeled internal synthesis.
