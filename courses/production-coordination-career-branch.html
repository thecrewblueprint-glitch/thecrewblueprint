Production & Coordination

This material is being held out of the normal learner catalog while the project decides how stage management, production management, venue operations, and coordination topics should be grouped for real-world learners.

The full prior course body is preserved in repository history and on archive/infrastructure-career-pre-owner-review-2026-09-06 so an authorized agent can retrieve it, compare it with the current role/employer research, and port selected material into an owner-review preview when requested.

The current research does not support presenting production work as one universal promotion ladder. Future material should describe real role families and context-specific responsibility rather than imply that every worker progresses through the same titles.
