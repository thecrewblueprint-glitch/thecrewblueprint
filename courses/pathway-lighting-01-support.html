Owner Review · Lighting Course 1

Lighting Support Awareness

A lighting support hand can help the department receive, route, stage, and organize assigned equipment without taking over electrical work, rigging, fixture setup, focus, addressing, networking, console operation, or system troubleshooting.

Support Starts With the Department Plan

The lighting lead or other designated authority controls the current build plan.

  • Recognize the lighting work area, case labels, staging locations, and lead.
  • Move or stage only the equipment included in the assignment.
  • Keep labels, protective parts, and organization intact unless the department directs otherwise.
  • Report visible damage, missing identification, or a changed condition before continuing.
  • Do not treat familiarity with a fixture, cable, control device, or hang position as permission to configure or operate it.

Communication Systems Vary

A venue, employer, department, or local crew may use its own radio language, verbal confirmations, hand signals, and emergency-stop system.

Learn the designated method for the task in front of you. Crew Blueprint does not declare one production signal universally controlling. When continuing becomes unclear or unsafe, stop your part of the task, alert the responsible person through the local system, and wait for a clear direction.

Stop / Alert / Escalate

Pause and get the responsible lighting person when equipment is damaged or unidentified, the destination is unclear, the local communication method is not understood, the task crosses into a specialized area, or the site condition no longer matches the instruction you received.

Knowledge Check

Does recognizing a lighting fixture authorize setup or configuration?

No. Recognition helps with support and routing; technical work remains with the assigned and authorized lighting crew.

Is one hand signal universal across every lighting crew?

No. Use the communication system designated for the current job and escalate when it is unclear.

What happens when the assignment crosses into electrical or rigging work?

Course 1 stops there. Get the qualified or authorized person responsible for that work.

Publication Boundary

This owner-review route teaches lighting-department literacy and support boundaries. It does not certify or authorize electrical work, rigging, climbing, fixture configuration, console operation, repair, testing, or other specialized tasks.
