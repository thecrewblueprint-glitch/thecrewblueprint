Field Tools, Jobsite Language, and Production Paperwork

The Crew Blueprint Resource Hub organizes practical jobsite tools, plain-language references, and the day-to-day paperwork used in live entertainment production.

This page is built for new and developing stagehands who need clearer language around the documents, checklists, and field references they'll run into on real calls.

Organizations Worth Knowing

A few real organizations worth knowing about — for training, research, professional development, and worker support beyond this site.

ESTA Technical Standards Program

Primary hub for ANSI-approved entertainment technology standards covering rigging, stage machinery, electrical systems, lighting, temporary structures, and related technical disciplines.

IATSE Training Trust Fund

Provides safety training curricula and industry-focused worker education, including OSHA 10/30 resources for entertainment workers.

USITT

Supports technical theatre research, education, professional development, and conferences focused on entertainment technology and performance production.

Support for Life on the Road

This work is hard on people, not just schedules. These organizations exist specifically to support the mental health, financial stability, and well-being of stagehands and touring crew.

Behind the Scenes Foundation

Provides health, safety, mental health, wellness, and support resources for entertainment industry workers.

The Roadie Clinic

A nonprofit built specifically for touring crew and their families — mental health services, career coaching, financial guidance, and recovery resources, in person and online.

Crew Nation

A relief fund for live event crew facing hardship — covers riggers, carpenters, sound and lighting techs, and other backstage roles, not just headline acts.
