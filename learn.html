You do not need to memorize the industry before your first call. Start with the shared jobsite foundation, then choose the lane that matches the work you want to understand.

Foundation

Start with the work environment.

Stagehand Fundamentals is the clearest existing beginner foundation in the corpus. It teaches call flow, crew behavior, safety mindset, communication, jobsite awareness, load-in/load-out, department boundaries, and career direction.

Four parts · 10 modules · 34 micro-lessons

Stagehand Fundamentals

Use this as the general jobsite foundation even if you later move directly into lighting, audio, video, staging, production, or another lane. It builds shared language and expectations; it does not make Stagehand the mandatory career root.
