What this curriculum is optimizing for

Useful at work. Safer at work.

Dependability

Understand the assignment, arrive prepared, communicate changes, respect handoffs, and finish the part of the job you actually own.

Transferable thinking

Recognize common patterns across lighting, audio, video, staging, shop, logistics, and production without flattening those departments into one job.

Boundaries

Know the difference between awareness, assigned support, system literacy, specialist work, and tasks that require explicit authorization or qualified personnel.

Why the library is small: the old 143-course universe remains preserved as historical evidence. This rebuild does not make learners navigate that archive. The new public curriculum packages the strongest recurring needs into a simpler sequence and moves deeper technical specialization into the advanced section.
