## Audit Methodology

This audit cross-referenced all major claims in Modules 1-10 against Research Packages 15-20/20A, which contain 40+ sources verified against real industry standards, employer practices, union guidance, and OSHA regulations. Verification method: each major claim was verified against at least one independent source. Claims without independent verification were flagged as unsupported or qualified.
